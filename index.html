GrabIt

Paste a link. Pick video or audio. Save it.

Download APK

Android 8.0 or newer. Size about 60 to 100 MB. Always the newest build.

What it does

Paste or share

Paste a link, or use the Android Share button in YouTube or Instagram and pick GrabIt.

Video or audio

Video in 360p, 480p, 720p, 1080p or best. Audio as MP3 or M4A.

Keeps going

Live progress, speed and time left. Downloads continue when you leave the app.

Queue and history

Add many links. Open, share or delete finished files from History.

Saved in your gallery

Files go to Movies, Music or Downloads. No storage permission on Android 10 and up.

Update engine

One tap updates yt-dlp inside the app, so it can keep up when sites change.

How to install

  1. Tap Download APK above.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. If Play Protect warns, see the fix below.
  4. Open GrabIt and accept the notice.
  5. Set Battery to Unrestricted: Settings, Apps, GrabIt, Battery, Unrestricted. This stops Android killing long downloads.

Read this first

  • Personal use only. Only save content you own or have permission to save.
  • Downloading from YouTube may break its Terms of Service. You are responsible for what you download.
  • GrabIt saves public videos only. It does not bypass logins, DRM or paywalls.
  • No analytics and no tracking. Nothing is uploaded. The app only makes normal requests to the video sites you ask for, and to GitHub when you tap Update engine.
  • This app is not on the Play Store. It is shared only by this page.

Problems and fixes

Play Protect blocks or warns on install
  1. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Play Protect, then the gear icon.
  3. Turn off scanning apps with Play Protect.
  4. Install GrabIt.
  5. Turn scanning back on.
"App not installed" when updating

Test builds change their signature on every build. Uninstall the old GrabIt first, then install the new one. Your saved files stay in your gallery. History is cleared.

A link stops working or says "site changed"

Sites change often, and Instagram and Facebook break more often than YouTube. Open Settings, tap Update engine, then try again. If it still fails, wait a day or two. The yt-dlp project usually fixes it fast.

"Private or needs a login"

GrabIt only saves public videos. Private, age-restricted and members-only videos cannot be saved.

Download stops when I leave the app

Set Battery to Unrestricted for GrabIt (Settings, Apps, GrabIt, Battery). Also allow notifications, so you can see and cancel downloads.

"Wi-Fi only is ON"

Connect to Wi-Fi, or turn the option off in GrabIt Settings.

The video will not play or share from History

The file may have been deleted or moved. Check Movies/GrabIt, Music/GrabIt or Downloads/GrabIt in your Files app.

"No internet" even though I am online

Turn Wi-Fi off and on, or switch to mobile data. Then try again.
